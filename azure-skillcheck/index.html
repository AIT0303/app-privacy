6. 子どものプライバシー

本アプリは個人情報を収集しないため、すべての年齢の方に安全にご利用いただけます。

Privacy Policy

App: Azure Skill Check
Provider: AITY Inc. (Kabushiki Kaisha AITY)
Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This app does not collect or transmit any personal information. All records — your skill check states, earned XP, levels, badges, titles, learning goals, and mission progress — are stored solely on your device. Neither the developer nor any third party can access this data.

2. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to any server or cloud; there is no account or login. Deleting the app removes all stored data.

3. Third Parties, Ads and Analytics

This app does not provide, sell, or share your information with any third party. In this version it performs no advertising, no analytics, and no behavioral tracking.

4. External Links

The app includes reference links to Microsoft's official documentation (Microsoft Learn) for each skill item. Tapping a link opens an external site in your device's browser. Please review the privacy policy of each destination site for how it handles information.

5. Permissions

The app does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. Network access is used only through standard device features when you open a documentation link or share your progress.

6. Children's Privacy

Because this app does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages.
